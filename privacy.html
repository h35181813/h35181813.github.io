Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Hermes is a private, personal assistant used only by its owner. It connects to one Google account that belongs to the assistant itself. No other users' data is collected.

What Google data it accesses

With the account holder's permission, Hermes can read, send and label email; read and change calendar events; read and change Drive files, Docs and Sheets; and read contacts in that one Google account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out the owner's requests, such as summarizing email or listing upcoming events. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Where it is stored and who it is shared with

The Google login token is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. When the owner asks Hermes to work with Google data, the relevant content is sent to a third-party AI model provider to generate a response. Data is not shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, or by deleting the token on the server.
